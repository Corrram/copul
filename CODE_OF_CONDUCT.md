# Code of Conduct

`copul` has adopted the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct. It applies to all project spaces (the GitHub
repository, its issues and pull requests, and the documentation) and to
anyone representing the project.

## Our standards

We want `copul` to be a welcoming and harassment-free project for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences,
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback, including on
  mathematical arguments,
- focusing on what is best for the community and its users.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- harassment, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political
  attacks,
- the use of sexualised language or imagery,
- publishing others' private information without their explicit permission,
- other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing or otherwise unacceptable behaviour may be
reported to the maintainer, Marcus Rockel, at
<marcus.rockel@finance.uni-freiburg.de>. All reports will be reviewed and
investigated promptly and fairly, and the privacy of the reporter will be
respected. The maintainer will follow the enforcement guidelines of the
Contributor Covenant (correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent ban).
